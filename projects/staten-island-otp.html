Staten Island Railway OTP Forecasting

An end-to-end, explainable machine learning system that analyzes, predicts, and forecasts Staten Island Railway on-time performance using real MTA Open Data.

GitHub Repo

Most transit reporting looks backward: it tells you what on-time performance was. This project asks what OTP will be and, just as importantly, why the model thinks so.

Built entirely on MTA Open Data for the Staten Island Railway, the project moves from historical trend analysis to machine-learning prediction, recursive multi-month forecasting, SHAP-based explainability, and operational risk scoring, all delivered through an interactive Streamlit dashboard styled for an executive audience.

It connects directly to my day job in transit operations analytics: turning raw reliability data into forward-looking, decision-ready insight.

Highlights

What it does.

📊

Executive overview

KPI cards for average OTP, average delay rate, and best/worst observed OTP give leadership the state of the railway at a glance.

📈

Historical trend analysis

Interactive OTP-over-time charts with sidebar filters for operational periods such as day-time categories.

🎯

Actual vs. predicted

Side-by-side comparison of real OTP against model predictions to evaluate how well the model tracks reality.

🔮

Recursive forecasting

A multi-month forecasting engine feeds each prediction back as a lag feature, projecting OTP 3–12 months ahead.

🚦

Operational risk scoring

Forecasted months are classified Low / Medium / High risk (≥95%, 90–95%, <90% OTP) with a plain-language outlook message.

🧠

Explainable AI with SHAP

SHAP values reveal the drivers behind each prediction such as seasonality, delay rate, recent OTP momentum, and rolling trends.

Approach

How it was built.

01

Data loading & review

Ingest Staten Island Railway OTP data from the MTA Open Data portal and audit quality and coverage.

02

Cleaning & EDA

Standardize the series, then explore seasonality, delay patterns, and long-run reliability trends.

03

Feature engineering

Build time-series features: OTP and delayed-train lags, rolling averages, delay rate, year, month, quarter, season, and day-time category.

04

Model training & selection

Benchmark Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost; XGBoost wins on structured, interaction-heavy tabular data.

05

Explainability

Apply SHAP to the final model so every forecast can answer not just “what?” but “why?”.

06

Forecasting & dashboard

Wrap the recursive forecaster, risk scoring, and visuals into a Streamlit app for interactive exploration.
