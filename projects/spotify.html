Spotify Top Tracks Dashboard

What makes a hit? An audio-feature analysis of Spotify's top tracks by danceability, energy, valence, acousticness, and streams, all in one dashboard.

Open in Tableau Public

Every track on Spotify carries a set of quantified audio attributes such as danceability, energy, valence, acousticness, instrumentalness, key. This dashboard puts those attributes side by side with streaming numbers for the platform's top tracks.

The result is a trend analysis of what today's most-streamed music actually sounds like in data: how energetic, how acoustic, how danceable and whether any of those attributes track with stream counts.
