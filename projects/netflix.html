Netflix Content Dashboard

An interactive exploration of the Netflix catalog — titles by type, country, rating, genre, and release year, down to individual show detail.

Open in Tableau Public

The Netflix catalog is a great visualization challenge: thousands of titles across dozens of countries, genres, ratings, and two decades of release years. This Tableau dashboard makes the whole catalog explorable in a single interactive view.

Maps show content by country of origin, while filters by type, rating, genre, and release year let you slice the catalog any way you want — down to detailed information on individual movies and TV shows.
