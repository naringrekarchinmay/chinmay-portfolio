Disease Progression ML

A planned healthcare analytics project on disease progression, multimorbidity patterns, and explainable risk prediction — bridging my PhD research interests with applied machine learning.

Get in touch

The next major project in this portfolio moves into healthcare analytics: modeling how diseases progress over time, how conditions cluster into multimorbidity patterns, and how risk can be predicted early enough to matter.

The emphasis will be on explainability and decision-support storytelling, the same philosophy behind my transit work. A risk score nobody can interpret doesn't change a clinical decision; a transparent one might.

This project connects directly to my doctoral research in information systems engineering management, where decision-support systems and applied analytics meet real operational and clinical environments.
