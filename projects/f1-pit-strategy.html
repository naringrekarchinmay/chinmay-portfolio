F1 Pit Strategy & Tyre Degradation

Can machine learning predict Formula 1 lap times? A race-analytics deep dive into the 2025 Monaco Grand Prix using FastF1 race pace, tyre degradation, and a lap-time model accurate to ~0.62 seconds.

GitHub Repo

Monaco is the most strategy-sensitive circuit on the calendar. Overtaking is nearly impossible, so lap time, tyre life, track position, pit timing, and traffic decide the race. That makes it the perfect testbed for a question: can machine learning predict race lap times from driver, tyre, and race-context features?

Using FastF1 data from the 2025 Monaco Grand Prix, this project cleans lap-level race data, analyzes pace by driver and compound, estimates tyre degradation on green-flag laps, engineers a full ML feature set, and trains competing regression models the best of which predicts lap times within about 0.62 seconds on average.

Everything is explorable in a six-page Streamlit dashboard, from race overview through an interactive lap-time predictor. The long-term goal is a multi-race strategy analytics system that can reason about pit windows and degradation across circuits.

Approach

How it was built.

01

Data collection

Pull the full 2025 Monaco GP race session with FastF1 laps, weather, and results.

02

Cleaning & EDA

Convert lap times to seconds, flag pit and abnormal laps, and chart race pace, compound usage, and driver consistency.

03

Tyre degradation

Estimate degradation from the tyre-life vs. lap-time relationship on green-flag laps only.

04

Feature engineering

Build features like race progress, tyre life (and its square), stint progress, driver/team median pace, previous lap, and rolling 3- and 5-lap averages.

05

Model training

Compare Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting on lap-time prediction.

06

Dashboard

Serve the trained Random Forest through a six-page Streamlit app, including a live-style lap-time predictor.
